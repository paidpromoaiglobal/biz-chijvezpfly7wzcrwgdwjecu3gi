About Us

Ghiladhari Tuition Centre Salim Raja Sir

Ghiladhari Tuition Centre, guided by the renowned Salim Raja Sir, is a trusted educational hub serving students across Ghiladhari, Rangagarakhuti, and the surrounding areas of Assam. With a strong commitment to academic excellence and personalized attention, the centre has become a go-to destination for students seeking quality education beyond the classroom. Our impressive 4.8-star rating reflects the trust and satisfaction of every student and parent who has been part of our learning community.

At our centre, we believe every student has the potential to excel with the right guidance and environment. Classes are conducted in two convenient sessions daily — early morning and evening — seven days a week, ensuring maximum flexibility for students of all schedules. Whether you are preparing for board exams or looking to strengthen your foundation in core subjects, Salim Raja Sir and our dedicated teaching approach are here to help you succeed.
